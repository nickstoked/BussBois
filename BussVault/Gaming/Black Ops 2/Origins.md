## Staffs Parts
---
*All staffs have same upgrade process after the unique steps listed below. Orb Puzzle in Excavation Site followed by placing and killing in the crazy place.*
### Fire
*Disc is around church or Gen 6. Upgrade by killing over the grates in the crazy place in front of portal and then doing the number/torch puzzle in the church*
- [ ] On-fire Airplane after opening church
- [ ] Church Gen 6
- [ ] Dropped from first Panzer
### Ice
*Disc is in the tank station. Upgrade by solving number puzzle in the crazy place and then freezing/shooting all of the tombstones*
- [ ] 3x dig spots while snowing
### Wind
*Disc is around Gen 4 and lightning tunnel. Upgrade by solving the rings in the crazy place and then shoot the smoke pillars towards The Excavation Site*
- [ ] 3x parts in the heads of the giants
### Lightning
*Disc is around Gen 5 and wind tunnel. Upgrade by solving the music puzzle in the crazy place and then turn all of the switches correctly.*
- [ ] First is jumping off of the tank on path towards tank station
- [ ] 2x parts by jumping off of the tank on the return path. (might technically be possible to get both in one go)
